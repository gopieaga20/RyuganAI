Strategy backtest 15-minute candles · option model
Pick one or more of the app's strategies, the symbols and the dates, and see how buying the ATM CE / PE on their calls would have done. Same rules as the app: a call is read on a candle's close and entered at the next candle's open, inside the entry window, with ADX at or above the minimum; one trade per symbol at a time; squared off at 15:15. Option prices are modelled (no historical option prices exist): ATM premium as a share of the price (0.55% for indices, about 2% for stocks scaled by each stock's volatility), delta 0.5, time decay, full option charges plus the bid-ask spread you set. A result is only worth something if it clearly beats the coin flip on the same candles.

Techno-Funda Screener F&O stocks · end of day
Fundamentals choose what to trade (quality and growth, from Yahoo Finance), technicals choose when (trend, relative strength, breakout volume and delivery). Runs nightly at 18:30 IST. Read this first: the technical rules alone were backtested on 4 years of daily data and showed no edge that can be told apart from chance (average +0.09 R per trade, about 17% likely to be luck). The fundamental rules cannot be backtested (no history exists), and promoter pledge, bank NPA and capital adequacy have no free data source, so they are not scored. Treat a high score as a shortlist to study, not a signal to follow.
