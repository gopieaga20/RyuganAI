# Technical gate of the techno-funda screener: 4-year backtest

Generated by `python tools/bt_screener.py`. **218 F&O stocks**, daily candles 2023-10-11 to 2026-11-13 (768 trading days after a 250-bar warm-up), benchmark Nifty 50. Rules are the ones the live screener uses (`backend/screener_tech.py`): close > 50-EMA > 200-EMA with a rising 200-EMA, RS rating > 80 (percentile of 3- and 6-month performance relative to Nifty 50 across the stocks), within 15% of the 52-week high; then a **breakout** (close above the 20-day high out of a base no wider than 15%, volume >= 1.5x the 20-day average, RSI <= 78, not more than 1 ATR above the entry) or a **pullback** (bullish reversal candle in the 20/50-EMA zone, RSI 45-60, T1 at least 1:2). Entry, stop, T1 (2R, or the swing high for pullbacks) and T2 (3R) follow blueprint section 5. The order is placed at the close and filled the next session only if price reaches it. Management: stop = whole position; T1 sells half and moves the stop to the entry; the rest trails under the 20-EMA or exits at T2; force-exit after 60 sessions. At most 10 positions at once, one per stock, strongest RS first. Costs 0.25% of the position per round trip. R = profit divided by the initial risk.

**What this does NOT test:** the fundamental gate and exchange delivery % (no point-in-time history exists), and the universe is today's F&O list, so stocks that were dropped or collapsed are missing (survivorship bias flatters long-only results). Data notes: {'too short / index': 1}.
